## Users

Podcast and sound professionals of Nouvelle-Aquitaine / Sud-Ouest de la France (journalistes, créateurs de podcast, compositeurs, ingénieurs son, monteurs) deciding whether to attend a one-day professional gathering. *[Inferred from LinkedIn/Instagram content: the event is explicitly billed "journée professionnelle" for this audience.]*

## Product Purpose

Landing page for PodOuest's first event, ÉPISODE 01, a one-day professional gathering for the podcast/sound industry of the Sud-Ouest. Success = a qualified visitor understands the day's program and clicks through to buy a ticket on HelloAsso.

## Positioning

The only regional gathering built specifically to connect Sud-Ouest podcast/sound professionals with each other (not a listener-facing podcast festival, not a generic media conference) — explicit mission stated by the founders: "faire exister l'écosystème qui est déjà là."

## Operating Context

Single standalone event page, no other pages/flows. External ticketing happens entirely on HelloAsso (out of scope to replicate). Founders promote via LinkedIn and Instagram; this page is the durable, linkable hub for both.

## Product Principles

1. Real content only — every claim traces to LinkedIn, Instagram, or the confirmed web-search result; nothing about the program, speakers, or venue is invented.
2. The HelloAsso event page is the single source of truth for price/registration — this page links out rather than duplicating unverifiable ticket details.
3. One clear action: get a qualified visitor to the HelloAsso ticket link.
4. Extend the confirmed identity rather than starting a new one — same hue family and glitch/waveform vocabulary as the association's own Instagram poster, raised to production craft.
